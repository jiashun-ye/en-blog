About

I created this academic blog to systematically document my research learning and exploration: from paper deep dives, reproduction and improvements, to structured course notes, and the full lifecycle of research projects—from inception to final wrap-up. It serves both as my periodic self-summary and as a window for readers interested in my research who want to follow its progress and details.

The main sections include: Paper Deep Dives (problem motivation, core contributions, key derivations, and clarifications), Reproduction & Extensions (experimental setup and implementation notes, comparative results, failure retrospectives, and directions for improvement), Course Notes (structured knowledge maps and distilled takeaways), and Research Project Logs (data and experimental design, interim conclusions, and open questions).

My writing principles are rigor, clarity, and reproducibility. In derivations and implementations, I aim to provide complete intermediate steps and the necessary background; when a result is not immediately obvious, I first offer an intuitive explanation to keep the main thread moving, and then unfold the proof or technical details separately, filling in the layers. When needed, I will link to relevant course notes on this site to support continuous reading.

Feedback and corrections are welcome. I also hope these records provide reusable reference materials for readers working on related topics or preparing to enter the field.

This site is available in both Chinese and English, updated simultaneously.

For more about me and the latest updates, please visit my homepage: jiashun-ye.github.io.
