  <p>This is Jiashun Ye's academic blog, The blog is organized into five sections:</p>

  <p><strong>Paper Breakdown</strong> — In-depth reading of selected papers, covering problem motivation, core contributions, and key derivations.</p>
  <p><strong>Literature Review</strong> — Broad surveys across a topic, comparing methods and tracing the development of ideas.</p>
  <p><strong>Foundations</strong> — Standalone notes on background knowledge, including mathematical tools, algorithms, and core concepts.</p>
  <p><strong>Research Log</strong> — An honest record of the research process: ideas, experiments, failures, and reflections.</p>
  <p><strong>Projects</strong> — Implementation notes on paper reproductions and self-built projects, with a focus on design decisions and lessons learned.</p>

  <p>This site is available in both Chinese and English, with content kept in sync as much as possible.</p>
